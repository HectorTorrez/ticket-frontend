## Space Buttons Apart

**Impact: MEDIUM (minimum 8pt; 16pt safer)**

Separate adjacent buttons by at least **8pt**; **16pt** is safer for destructive/next-to-primary pairs.

**Incorrect:**

"Cancel" and "Delete" touching with no gap on mobile.

**Correct:**

Gap token `sm` (16px) between horizontal button groups. Stack on narrow viewports if needed.

Reference: [Button design tips](https://www.adhamdannaway.com/blog/ui-design/button-design-tips)
