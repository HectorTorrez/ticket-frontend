## Keep Important Content Visible

**Impact: HIGH (overflow menus hide discoverability)**

Hiding actions in menus saves space but risks missed tasks. If space allows, surface important actions directly.

**Incorrect:**

"Share" and "Bookmark" only inside a kebab menu on a profile with ample horizontal space.

**Correct:**

Show frequent/important actions as visible secondary buttons or icon+label controls. Reserve overflow for rare actions.
