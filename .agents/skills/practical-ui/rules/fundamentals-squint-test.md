## Use the Squint Test

**Impact: HIGH (catches weak hierarchy before ship)**

Squint, blur the design, or step back from the screen. You should still recognize the page purpose and see which element is most important.

**Incorrect:**

After squinting, everything blends together; no obvious primary action or title.

**Correct:**

Primary button/title remains the dominant shape. Secondary content recedes. Adjust size, contrast, or weight until hierarchy reads at a glance.
