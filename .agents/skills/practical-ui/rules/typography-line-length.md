## Keep Line Length at 40–80 Characters

**Impact: HIGH (measure affects reading speed and fatigue)**

Body text should be **40–80 characters per line** (including spaces). Too long: hard to find the next line. Too short: excessive eye travel.

**Incorrect:**

Full-width paragraphs on desktop (120+ characters). Narrow sidebar text at 25 characters.

**Correct:**

Constrain prose with `max-width: 65ch` (or ~640–720px depending on size). Responsive columns that preserve measure.

Reference: [Practical UI](https://www.practical-ui.com/)
