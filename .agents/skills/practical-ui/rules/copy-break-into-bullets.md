## Break Content with Headings and Bullets

**Impact: MEDIUM (chunking aids comprehension)**

Split large blocks into smaller pieces with descriptive subheadings and bullet lists for parallel items.

**Incorrect:**

Three dense paragraphs covering cancellation, refunds, and support in one block.

**Correct:**

Three subsections with headings, or a bulleted list of policies. One idea per chunk.

Reference: [Practical UI](https://www.practical-ui.com/)
