## Base Decisions on Logic, Not Opinion

**Impact: CRITICAL (every detail should be defensible)**

When choosing layout, spacing, typography, or colour, articulate **why** a choice improves usability, accessibility, or scannability. Avoid "I like it" or "it looks pretty" as the only rationale.

**Incorrect:**

Moving elements until they "feel right" without checking contrast, grouping, or hierarchy.

**Correct:**

"This heading uses bold at 20px because it's the page title (hierarchy). Body is regular 16px with 1.5 line-height for readability. Spacing between sections is 32px (md token) because they're unrelated groups."

Reference: [Practical UI](https://www.practical-ui.com/)
