## Headings Must Work Out of Context

**Impact: HIGH (screen readers list all headings)**

Many users scan heading lists or hear headings in isolation. Each heading should make sense without surrounding body text.

**Incorrect:**

Heading "More" with details only in the paragraph below.

**Correct:**

"More about your plan" or "Additional features". Avoid "Click here" patterns in headings.

Reference: [Practical UI](https://www.practical-ui.com/)
