## Use Descriptive Headings

**Impact: HIGH (headings are scan anchors)**

Headings should summarize the section content—not vague labels like "Overview" when "Shipping options" is clearer.

**Incorrect:**

Generic heading "Details" above mixed shipping and payment info.

**Correct:**

Specific headings: "Delivery address", "Payment method". Users grasp structure without reading paragraphs.
