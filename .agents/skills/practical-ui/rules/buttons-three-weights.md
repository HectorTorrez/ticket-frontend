## Define Three Button Weights

**Impact: HIGH (communicates action importance)**

Most interfaces need three button styles:

| Weight | Use | Typical style |
|--------|-----|---------------|
| **Primary** | Single most important action | Filled, high contrast |
| **Secondary** | Alternative or equal secondary actions | Outline or muted fill |
| **Tertiary** | Low-priority or destructive (de-emphasized) | Text/link style |

Hierarchy must differ by **shape/fill**, not colour alone.

**Incorrect:**

Primary and secondary both filled rectangles differing only by blue vs grey hue.

**Correct:**

Primary: solid fill. Secondary: outline with ≥3:1 border. Tertiary: text with underline or clear tap area.
