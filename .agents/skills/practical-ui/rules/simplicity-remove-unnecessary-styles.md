## Remove Unnecessary Styles

**Impact: HIGH (every line and colour should earn its place)**

Avoid decorative borders, backgrounds, dividers, and animations that don't convey structure or state. Unnecessary chrome distracts and increases cognitive load.

**Incorrect:**

Double borders around images, redundant box shadows, coloured headings on non-interactive text, gradient backgrounds behind every section.

**Correct:**

Remove chrome that doesn't group or emphasize. Use whitespace and typography for structure first.
