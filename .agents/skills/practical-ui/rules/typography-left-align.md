## Left-Align Body Text

**Impact: MEDIUM (center/justified body hurts scan speed)**

English and other LTR languages read in an F-pattern. Left-align long body text. Center works for short headings or labels; avoid centered or justified paragraphs.

**Incorrect:**

Center-aligned product description paragraphs. Fully justified text with uneven word spacing.

**Correct:**

`text-align: left` for body. Center only for hero titles ≤2 lines or numeric stat blocks.
