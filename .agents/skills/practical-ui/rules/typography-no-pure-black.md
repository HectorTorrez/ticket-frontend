## Avoid Pure Black Text

**Impact: MEDIUM (extreme contrast fatigues readers)**

Pure black (`#000`) on white creates maximum contrast and can cause eye strain in long reading. Use dark grey for body text; reserve near-black for emphasis.

**Incorrect:**

`color: #000` for all text on `#fff` backgrounds.

**Correct:**

Body: `#374151`–`#1f2937` range (still ≥4.5:1). Titles slightly darker. De-emphasized text lighter grey (still meet contrast minimums).
