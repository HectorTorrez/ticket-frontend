## Prefer Single Alignment

**Impact: MEDIUM (mixed alignment increases scan cost)**

Each extra alignment type (left, center, right) forces eyes to jump. Within a component or section, stick to one alignment—usually left for LTR content.

**Incorrect:**

Center-aligned tabs above left-aligned body and right-aligned metadata in one card.

**Correct:**

Left-align tabs with body content. Center alignment only for short hero headings or empty states—not long body text.
